Brazil

Q3/Q4 2026 Edition
Politics

1. Lula’s 3rd Term Mid-Point

Government focuses on fiscal balance, social programs, and Amazon protection. Tensions with Congress over budget amendments continue.

2. Municipal Election Fallout

2024 local results still shape alliances. Centrão parties gain influence in Congress ahead of 2026 general elections.

Security & Law

3. Security + Amazon Policy

Federal task forces expanded to fight deforestation and organized crime. New bill on regulating social media passes first round.

Economy

4. Growth Stabilizes

GDP forecast ~2.4%. Driven by agriculture, services, and oil. Inflation drops to ~3.9%.

5. Interest Rates Cut

Central Bank brings Selic rate down to 10.25% to boost investment and consumption.

Fiscal & Energy

6. Fiscal Framework Tested

New spending rules hold, but pressure rises for more investment in infrastructure and education.

7. Energy Exports Boom

Record oil + ethanol exports. Brazil pushes to become major green hydrogen exporter by 2030.

Technology

8. AI + Digital Gov

Federal "AI Strategy 2026" launched. Gov.br expands AI chatbots for public services.

9. 5G + Semiconductors

Full 5G rollout in all state capitals. New tax incentives attract chip and data center investments in São Paulo and Ceará.

Fintech & Climate

10. Fintech & Pix 2.0

Pix adds offline payments and international transfers. Brazil leads LatAm in digital payments and open banking.

11. Climate Tech

Major funding for startups in agrotech, carbon credits, and biofuels. COP30 prep in Belém boosts green tech sector.

Canada

Q3/Q4 2026 Edition
Politics & Trade

1. Politics

PM Mark Carney’s Liberal government faces pressure over cost of living and housing ahead of post-2025 election dynamics.

2. US Relations

Trade tensions over US tariffs on steel, aluminum, and autos. Canada retaliates and seeks new trade partners.

Economy & Housing

3. Economy

Inflation near 2.2%. Bank of Canada keeps rates high but is slowly cutting to avoid recession.

4. Housing

Housing crisis continues. Federal plan to build 3.9M homes by 2031 + limits on foreign buyers.

Energy & Fiscal

5. Energy

TMX pipeline expansion advances. Debate continues between oil/gas and green transition.

6. Fiscal

Federal deficit around CAD $40B. Focus on spending cuts and revenue growth.

AI Ecosystem

7. AI Ecosystem

$2.4B CAD invested in AI infrastructure and talent. Toronto and Montreal lead as global AI hubs.

Semiconductors

8. Semiconductors

Major subsidies to attract chip and battery plants from Tesla, VW, and Northvolt.

Clean Tech & Digital

9. Clean Tech

Push on green hydrogen and critical minerals. Canada positions itself as key supplier to US/EU.

10. Digital

Online News Act in effect. Meta/Google pay Canadian media. National digital ID expanding.
